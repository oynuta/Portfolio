A little about me

Hi! I'm Oymonjon Ro‘zmetova, an 11th-grade student from historic Khiva, Uzbekistan, with a deep curiosity for technology, software development, education, and social innovation.

I'm passionate about using technology to solve real-world problems and building projects that bring genuine value to people. Alongside my journey in IT, I actively participate in volunteering, coordinate youth and STEM initiatives, and thrive on collaborating with ambitious learners.
